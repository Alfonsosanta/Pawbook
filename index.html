Terms of Service & Privacy Policy 📄

1. Community Content Guidelines

Pawbook is dedicated exclusively to pets and animals. All uploaded photos and media must feature real pets or animals. Human selfies, landscape images, and unrelated non-pet posts are strictly prohibited and will be removed.

2. Automated Media Validation

To preserve community standards, Pawbook utilizes algorithmic image verification. Uploading non-pet media or landscape scenery will trigger automatic blocking and content moderation notifications.

3. Data Privacy & Account Security

We respect your privacy. Pawbook uses secure Firebase Authentication for account verification and email security. Personal registration details are encrypted and never shared with third parties.

4. Safety & Content Moderation

Posts reported by users for guidelines violations will be automatically hidden, and the author will receive official removal notices. Repeat violations may result in account termination.
